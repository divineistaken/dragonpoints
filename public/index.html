Field guide

How the ledger works

  1. Earn points. Show up to events, win brackets, help members and keep your check-in streak alive.
  2. Climb the board. The leaderboard switches between all-time, this month and this week. Ties break by the longer streak.
  3. Unlock achievements. Each one pays a one-time bonus and is tracked on your profile.
  4. Vote in polls. Council polls carry extra weight. Results are published here once voting closes.

Points are recorded by the guild staff. Spotted a mistake? Open a ticket in the server.
